# Meta Intel Technologies

## Field Operations & Retail Execution Platform

A centralized field operations management platform designed for a Through-the-Line (TTL) marketing agency.

The platform consists of:

- A web application for management, operations, supervisors, analysts, and authorized client users.
- A mobile application for field teams such as merchandisers, brand ambassadors, promoters, and field representatives.
- A centralized Supabase backend for authentication, database, storage, security, and server-side functionality.

## Core Capabilities

- Client and campaign management
- Field team management
- Locations and route management
- Assignments and scheduling
- Attendance and shift tracking
- Field visits
- Retail audits and OSA
- Sales capture
- Photo and evidence collection
- Issues and escalation
- Dashboards and reporting
- Audit logging
- Offline-first mobile data collection and synchronization
